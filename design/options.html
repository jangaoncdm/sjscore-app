SJGP field app · five directions

The same two screens and the same words in each. What changes is the system: shape, type, colour, navigation, where the primary action sits, and how the welcome screen moves. The emblem on every welcome is the Government of Telangana seal the app already ships; Replay runs the motion again. Pick one by name and the build follows it screen by screen.

SJGP field app · flow

Tap through on the phone. Every screen has one primary action, the bottom bar never leaves, and the day's status is always the first thing on Today.

  1. Sign in once: number and PIN, then the phone remembers the officer.
  2. Today opens on the day's status. If attendance is not marked, the only thing that matters is the card at the top.
  3. Mark attendance: the location is read four times while the photograph is taken. Identical readings bring up the walk-and-read-again sheet. Out of place brings up the fence sheet.
  4. Confirm shows the stamped photograph and the two checks that passed before the mark goes.
  5. Villages lists what is due this reporting month, then the score sheet one section at a time, then review and file.
  6. Records is a calendar month, with leave applied for from the same place.
  7. Notices hold circulars to acknowledge and any show-cause notice on file.
  8. Offline is a state, not a screen: a banner and a queue, never an error.

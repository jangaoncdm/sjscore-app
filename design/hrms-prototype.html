Decisions this draft takes, for you to confirm

  1. Six kinds of leave on the rail: casual, earned, half pay, optional holiday, child care (women), medical, and the headquarters permission as a request and not a balance. The live app carries four.
  2. The journey shows two desks: the controlling officer in C1, then the sanctioning authority in C2 or C3. Your order of 08.10 sends everything to you; the Base Format says casual leave is mostly the head of office's. The draft draws the form the data has. Say which.
  3. The heat map is the leave one, in two sizes: his own twelve months, and the office's calendar a month at a time, on the same five-step ramp as the console. A fifty-three-week strip does not fit a phone.
  4. Earned and half pay leave are balances at credit, taken from the Service Register as verified, and the app debits from them. It does not compute credit accrual; that stays the office's.

What the files need before they are loaded

  • 51 mobile numbers are not ten digits; 10 numbers appear on two rows; 46 rows have no employee id.
  • 190 rows leave the casual-leave figure blank and 183 the balance date, mostly the outsourced and contract staff, which the Field Guide allows.
  • Two workbooks answer in free text where a list was asked: "Yes" for present status, a date in the sanctioning-authority column.
  • The State Audit workbook is an old .xls and is read separately.

Gram Palana Register — Privacy Policy

Collectorate, Jangaon District, Telangana · Effective September 2026

The Gram Palana Register is an official application of the Jangaon District administration, Revenue department. It is used by authorised government functionaries — Gram Palana Officers, Assistant and Mandal Revenue Inspectors — to record attendance and leave. It does not carry the sanitation evaluation, which is a separate register. It is not available to the general public; sign-in is limited to mobile numbers registered by the District administration.

What the app collects

The officer's mobile number, name, role, mandal and the revenue villages in his charge, as registered by the District administration. Attendance: the day, the time the mark was made and the time the district received it. Device location, captured only at the moment the officer presses the mark button, so that the attendance mark carries the place it was made from and its accuracy. No photograph is taken and no inspection data is collected on this register.

How it is used and stored

Data is used solely for attendance and leave administration by the District administration. The distance between a mark and the officer's place of duty is shown to the District for information; it raises no notice, debits no leave and locks no account. Records are held on the officer's device until synced, then stored in systems controlled by the District administration. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, except as required for official purposes or by law.

Your choices

Location permission is requested by the operating system and may be declined; the mark is then recorded without a place. Requests for correction of records, or for deletion of an account, are handled by the District administration.

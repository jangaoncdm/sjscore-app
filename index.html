Attendance

Mark today’s attendance

What is recorded. One photograph, one set of coordinates and the time, at the moment you press Mark attendance — nothing else, and nothing at any other time. The app does not follow your movements during the day and holds no background location. Attendance can be marked without a network; it is kept on the phone and goes to the district when signal returns.

Show cause notice

Acknowledgement required

The app remains locked until every pending notice below is acknowledged. Acknowledgement records receipt; your explanation travels with it to the Collector.

The rule. On working days (Sundays and declared holidays excepted), unmarked attendance draws a show-cause notice at mid-morning. The first three notices in a calendar month stand as warnings; from the next one on, if attendance is still unmarked at the close of the day, one day of Casual Leave is debited — loss of pay once the year’s CL is exhausted. Marking attendance is the cure; acknowledgement alone does not stop the debit.

Add SJGP to your Home Screen

On iPhone this must be done from Safari. It takes two taps, and the app then opens full screen and works offline.

  1. Tap Share — the square with an arrow, at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open the app from the green SJ icon after that.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Open this same address in Safari instead — only Safari can add it to the Home Screen.

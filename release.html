The district register — release record

Everything the register has been given since the district adopted it on 20 July 2026, release by release, with what each one was for. Built from the repository’s own history and from the live registers on 10 October 2026.

What it is

Two registers, each its own Apps Script project over its own spreadsheet, serving the same application from one address. Officers mark attendance and file their returns from their phones, often on weak rural signal; the Collector reads both from a single console. What it writes is a government record — it is used to issue show-cause notices under the Telangana Civil Services (Conduct) Rules 1964 and to debit casual leave — which is why the rules further down are written the way they are.

The rules that were paid for

Each of these is a failure that reached officers in the field. They are recorded here because they are the reason the register behaves as it does, and because undoing one of them would repeat it.

  • The phone’s clock is not evidence. A handset eleven minutes fast was recording marks in the future. Compliance is judged on the earlier of the officer’s claim and the district’s receipt.
  • A mark on the phone is not a mark the district has. The day is read at 18:00, and attendance is read three times before a day’s leave is debited.
  • Dates from the sheet are not strings. A spreadsheet silently transposes day and month when both are twelve or less. It put nine holidays on the wrong date, and in October 2026 it closed a working day across the district.
  • The server decides; the console only asks. Every write re-checks role, ownership and balance.
  • Nothing is destroyed. Withdrawn evaluations, superseded schedules, retired officers and reversed debits all remain on the register. A file can be produced afterwards.
  • Idempotence everywhere. Every job runs twice without changing anything the second time.
  • Silence under sanction is leave, not absence. Anything counting the unmarked consults the leave register first.
  • “Verified” is not “present at the place of duty.” It means only that the handset returned a precise fix. The distance is printed and accuses nobody.
  • One handset is one officer. Two officers sharing a phone must never inherit each other’s marks or unsent work.
  • A plan of work is not a charge. Falling behind the filing schedule, a missing development plan and an unacknowledged circular raise no notice, no debit and no lock.

How to read this

Each line is one release: a change that passed the suites, was published and was deployed to the district the same day. The headings above each group say what that period of work was for; the lines beneath are what was actually delivered. Reversals are not listed, because a change that was taken back was never given.

Nothing in this document is typed by hand except the ten headings and the rules above. The releases are read out of the repository’s history, the figures out of the passes that produced them, and the state of each register out of the register itself at the moment this was built. It carries no officer’s name, no mobile number and no PIN.

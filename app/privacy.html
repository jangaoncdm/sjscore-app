SJGP — Privacy Policy

Collectorate, Jangaon District, Telangana · Effective July 2026

SJGP (Swachh Jangaon Gram Panchayat) is an official application of the Jangaon District administration. It is used by authorised government functionaries to record the monthly sanitation evaluation of Gram Panchayats under the Swachh Jangaon Sanitation Task Force framework. It is not available to the general public; sign-in is limited to mobile numbers registered by the District Panchayat Office.

What the app collects

The officer's mobile number, name, role and jurisdiction, as registered by the District Panchayat Office. Inspection data: marks awarded, red-flag events, registers referenced and remarks. Device location, captured only when the officer presses the capture button or takes a photograph, so that an inspection can be geo-stamped. Photographs of public sanitation sites — drains, waste facilities, roads and public buildings — taken by the officer during the inspection.

How it is used and stored

Data is used solely for statutory sanitation monitoring, grading and administrative review by the District administration. Records are held on the officer's device until synced, then stored in systems controlled by the District Panchayat Office. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, except as required for official purposes or by law.

Your choices

Location and camera permissions are requested by the operating system and may be declined; the related features are then unavailable. Requests for correction of records, or for deletion of an account, are handled by the District Panchayat Office.

Contact

District Panchayat Officer, Collectorate, Jangaon District, Telangana.

Government of Telangana · Jangaon District

District HRMS — install the app on your phone

One address for every phone. Apply for leave, see what the year has left you, and track every application to the District Magistrate’s order — from your own handset.

The address, in words

https://jangaoncdm.github.io/sjscore-app/hrms/

Scan the square with the phone’s camera, or type the address into the browser. Both open the same page. Nothing is installed from a shop: the app is this page, kept on your home screen.

Installing

Android

Chrome — the browser most phones already have

  1. 1
    Open the address in Chrome.Scan the square with the camera and tap the link, or type the address.
  2. 2
    Tap “Add to Home Screen” on the page.It is on the Home screen and under Me. If you do not see it, open Chrome’s menu (the three dots, top right) and tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. 3
    Tap Install.The HRMS icon appears among your apps. Open it from there from now on.

iPhone

Safari only — Chrome on an iPhone cannot add it

  1. 1
    Open the address in Safari.Scan the square with the Camera app and tap the link. If it opens in another browser, copy the address and paste it into Safari.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share button.The square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (or at the top on an iPad).
  3. 3
    Scroll the sheet and tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.The HRMS icon appears on the Home Screen. Open it from there from now on — not from Safari.
The first time, open the app where there is a signal. After that it opens without one, and anything you send while offline goes up when the line returns.

Signing in for the first time

  1. 1
    Open HRMS from the home screen and enter your mobile number.The number your office holds for you on the establishment. Tap Sign in.
  2. 2
    Enter the code your office gave you.A six-character enrolment code from your office’s sheet — or, where the sheet says so, your employee id. It is used once and then spent.
  3. 3
    Choose your own PIN — 4 to 8 digits — and enter it twice.Nobody else knows it. You sign in with your number and this PIN from then on.
  4. 4
    You are in.Home shows what you have, what is coming and what needs you. Request opens an application; every application shows its journey to the District Magistrate’s order.

If something does not work

  1. ?
    “This number is not on the establishment.”Your office has not sent your row, or holds a different number for you. Ask your office; it checks the number and the employee id against your name.
  2. ?
    The code is refused.A code opens one row once. If it was used already, or typed from a smudged sheet, your office asks the Collectorate for the row to be looked at.
  3. ?
    No “Add to Home Screen” on an iPhone.You are not in Safari. Copy the address into Safari and try again.

This square and this address are not a credential. Anyone who photographs them reaches the sign-in page and gets no further without a number on the establishment and the code or PIN that goes with it. Put them on the notice board.  ·  Issued by the Collectorate, Jangaon, for the offices of the district establishment.
